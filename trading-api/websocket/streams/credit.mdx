Streams your current credit utilisation and limit. Subscribe to this if you trade
on credit — an order that would breach the limit is rejected with
`OrderExceedsLimit`.

## Subscribe

```json Client → server
{
  "reqid": 25,
  "type": "subscribe",
  "streams": [{ "name": "Exposure" }]
}
```

### Parameters

None.

## Response

<ResponseField name="ExposureCurrency" type="string" required>
  The currency all exposure values are expressed in.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="Exposure" type="decimal" required>
  Current credit utilisation, in `ExposureCurrency`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="ExposureLimit" type="decimal" required>
  Credit limit, in `ExposureCurrency`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="Status" type="string" required>
  `Online` or `Offline`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="Timestamp" type="datetime" required>
  When credit was last updated.
</ResponseField>

## Reading it

Headroom is simply:

```
headroom = ExposureLimit − Exposure
```

Both figures are in `ExposureCurrency`, which is **not** necessarily the currency
of the order you are about to send. A limit expressed in USD constrains a
EUR-denominated order by its USD equivalent, so convert before comparing — see
[`CurrencyConversion`](/trading-api/websocket/streams/currency#currencyconversion)
or request `EquivalentCurrency` on the
[`Balance`](/trading-api/websocket/streams/balance) stream.

<Tip>
Check headroom before submitting rather than handling an `OrderExceedsLimit`
rejection afterwards. Pre-checking lets you **size the order down** to what fits;
a rejection just loses the order and you start again with a stale view of the
market.
</Tip>

## Exposure is not balance

These answer different questions and are easy to conflate.

| | Measures | Ask it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`Balance`](/trading-api/websocket/streams/balance) | Funds you actually hold | Sizing against settled assets |
| `Exposure` | Credit consumed against a limit | Trading on credit, before settlement |

An account can hold a healthy `AvailableAmount` and still be refused for
`OrderExceedsLimit`, because the constraint is the credit line rather than the
cash. The reverse also happens.

## When the stream goes Offline

<Warning>
When `Status` is `Offline` the figures are **stale**, and the platform is telling
you so explicitly. Do not gate order submission on a stale exposure number —
either pause trading or fall back to your own conservative estimate until the
stream returns to `Online`.

`Timestamp` tells you how stale. A number that has not moved while you have been
trading is a much stronger signal of a feed problem than of genuinely flat
utilisation.
</Warning>

## Credit limits over REST

Point-in-time credit and configured limits are also available at
`GET /v1/customer-credit` and `GET /v1/customer-credit/limits` — see
[REST endpoints](/trading-api/rest/endpoints#credit). Use the stream for
pre-trade checks and REST for reporting; polling REST in a trading loop is slower
and gives you a staler answer than the stream you could have subscribed to.

<Note>
Whether you trade on credit at all, and the size of the line, are commercial
terms set for your account. If `ExposureLimit` is not what you expect, that is a
conversation with your account manager rather than an API issue.
</Note>
