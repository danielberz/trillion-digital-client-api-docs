<Steps>
  <Step title="Commercial discussion">
    Scope of trading, instruments, expected volumes, and which Trillion Digital
    entity you will face. Contact
    [sales@trilliondigital.io](mailto:sales@trilliondigital.io) or your account
    manager.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Onboarding and verification">
    Entity documentation, beneficial ownership, and KYC/KYB checks. Timelines
    depend on structure complexity and document turnaround. See
    [Onboarding](/guides/accounts/onboarding).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Account activation">
    Once approved, your trading account is provisioned and platform users are
    invited by email. See [Users and roles](/guides/accounts/users-and-roles).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Register settlement details">
    Register the bank accounts you will settle to and whitelist wallet addresses
    for crypto. **Do this before your first trade** — settlement cannot complete
    against unregistered destinations. See
    [Settlement instructions](/guides/settlement/settlement-instructions),
    [Bank account whitelisting](/guides/settlement/bank-accounts), and
    [Wallet whitelisting](/guides/settlement/wallet-whitelisting).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose how you will trade">
    Four channels carry the same liquidity — the high-touch desk, chat, the
    portal, and the API. See
    [Execution channels](/guides/trading/execution-channels) for which fits your
    flow.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fund and trade">
    Move funds in, request a quote, and trade. See
    [Trade lifecycle](/guides/trading/trade-lifecycle).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If you are integrating

Request **sandbox** API credentials from
[support@trilliondigital.io](mailto:support@trilliondigital.io) as early as
possible — sandbox access does not depend on your funding being in place, so
development can run in parallel with onboarding.

<Warning>
Sandbox and production are entirely separate: separate credentials, separate
data, separate hosts. Certify your integration against sandbox before requesting
production keys. See [Environments](/trading-api/environments).
</Warning>
