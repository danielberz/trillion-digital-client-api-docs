Trillion Digital distributes the same liquidity through four channels. They are
not tiers, and most clients use more than one: a desk might run systematic flow
over the API and pick up the phone for a block that needs working.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="High-touch OTC" icon="phone">
    Deal directly with a trader. For size, illiquid pairs, and anything that
    needs to be worked or structured.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chat" icon="comments">
    Request and accept quotes from Telegram, Slack, or WhatsApp. Conversational,
    with an audit trail.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Portal" icon="display">
    The web terminal. Self-service RFQ, blotter, balances, and statements. No
    development required.
  </Card>
  <Card title="API" icon="code">

    systematic flow.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Choosing a channel

| | High-touch | Chat | Portal | API |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Best for** | Large or complex trades | Ad-hoc dealing on the move | Day-to-day manual trading | Automation, systematic flow |
| **Price discovery** | Negotiated with a trader | RFQ | RFQ and streaming | RFQ and streaming |
| **Setup required** | None | Channel provisioning | Login | Integration work |
| **Human in the loop** | Yes | Yes, when needed | No | No |
| **Available** | Desk hours | Desk hours | 24/7 | 24/7 |

<Note>
Pricing is not a function of the channel. The same trade in the same size prices
the same whether it is requested by phone, chat, portal, or API — the channel
determines convenience and workflow, not economics.
</Note>

## High-touch OTC

Speak or message directly with a trader. This is the right channel when the trade
is large enough that execution strategy matters, when the pair is thin, when you
want the trade worked over a period rather than filled at a point, or when the
structure is not a simple spot exchange.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reach the desk">
    Through your account manager or the dealing channel established during
    onboarding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discuss the trade">
    Instrument, size, direction if you wish to disclose it, and any constraints
    on how it should be executed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive a price">
    Quoted for your specific size.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    On agreement the trade is booked and a confirmation is issued, exactly as
    with any other channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
If a quote you can see in the portal or over the API looks wrong for the size you
want, that is precisely the moment to call the desk rather than trade on it.
Screen liquidity is not the whole of what the desk can do.
</Tip>

## Chat

Quote and trade from Telegram, Slack, or WhatsApp, in a channel provisioned for
your account. Useful when you are away from a terminal, and popular with
treasury teams who want dealing to sit in the same place as the rest of their
operational conversation.

Typical commands cover requesting a quote, booking, modifying and cancelling,
listing available pairs, and retrieving settlement instructions.

<Note>
Send `/help` in your channel for the current command set — it is authoritative
and reflects exactly what your account is entitled to, which a documentation page
cannot.
</Note>

<Warning>
Chat channels are provisioned per account and per group. If your group is
upgraded or migrated by the messaging platform, the channel identifier can change
and the bot will stop responding — tell us rather than assuming the service is
down.
</Warning>

## Portal

The web terminal at
[trading.trilliondigital.io](https://trading.trilliondigital.io), with sandbox at
`sandbox.trading.trilliondigital.io`. Request quotes, trade, and review your
blotter, balances, and statements.

Access is provisioned during onboarding — see
[Users and roles](/guides/accounts/users-and-roles). Every user is named and
enrols multi-factor authentication.

## Settlement is the same everywhere

Whichever channel executes the trade, settlement runs the same way: against your
registered [settlement instructions](/guides/settlement/settlement-instructions),
[bank accounts](/guides/settlement/bank-accounts), and
[whitelisted wallets](/guides/settlement/wallet-whitelisting).

<Warning>
Trading through a new channel does not re-open settlement setup, and it does not
create a route to pay a destination that has not been registered. A trade agreed
by phone still settles only to an approved destination.
</Warning>
