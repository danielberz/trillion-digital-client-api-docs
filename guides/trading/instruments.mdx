The instruments available to you depend on your account's entitlements, your
facing entity, and your commercial terms.

<Note>
**Your account's live instrument list is authoritative.** In the terminal it is
the instrument selector; over the API it is the
[`Security`](/trading-api/websocket/streams/security) stream or
`GET /v1/markets`. Query it rather than maintaining a hardcoded list — securities
are added and disabled over time.
</Note>

## Symbol convention

Instruments are quoted as a pair, `BASE-QUOTE`:

| Symbol | Base | Quote | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `BTC-USD` | BTC | USD | Bitcoin priced in US dollars |
| `ETH-USD` | ETH | USD | Ether priced in US dollars |
| `USDT-USD` | USDT | USD | Tether priced in US dollars |

Buying `BTC-USD` means receiving BTC and paying USD.

## Constraints on every instrument

Each security carries its own trading constraints, published on the
[`Security`](/trading-api/websocket/streams/security) stream:

<ResponseField name="MinimumSize / MaximumSize">
  The smallest and largest order accepted.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="MinPriceIncrement">
  Prices must be a whole multiple of this.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="MinSizeIncrement">
  Quantities expressed in the **base** currency must be a whole multiple of this.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="MinAmtIncrement">
  Quantities expressed in the **quote** currency must be a whole multiple of this.
</ResponseField>

<Warning>
Increment violations are the most common cause of rejected orders. Note that
which increment applies depends on the currency you expressed the order in.
Round before submitting.
</Warning>

## Settlement currency

An instrument's settlement currency is not always its quote currency. Check
`SettlementCurrency` on the security, and make sure you hold registered
settlement details for it — see
[Settlement instructions](/guides/settlement/settlement-instructions).

## Adding an instrument

If you need something not currently enabled on your account, ask your account
manager. Availability depends on liquidity, your entity, and compliance review.
