Trillion Digital is a principal counterparty. You trade bilaterally with us at a
price quoted to you, rather than matching anonymously against other participants
on a public order book.

## Why it differs from an exchange

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Price is quoted to you" icon="tag">
    You receive a firm price in the size you asked for. There is no partial
    walk-up through a book and no slippage from your own order moving the market.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Size is private" icon="eye-slash">
    Your interest is not broadcast. Large orders do not signal to the market
    before they execute.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settlement is bilateral" icon="building-columns">
    Funds move between your accounts and ours against agreed instructions — not
    through an exchange's omnibus wallet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Credit is a relationship" icon="handshake">
    Where credit is extended, terms are set for your account rather than
    uniformly across a venue.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The two execution styles

### Request for quote

The normal path. You ask for a price in a specific size; we return a firm two-way
quote valid for a short window; you choose whether to trade. See
[RFQ workflow](/guides/trading/rfq-workflow).

Best when size matters and you want certainty of price before committing.

### Streaming prices with orders

Continuous prices, against which you place market or limit orders. See
[Order commands](/trading-api/websocket/commands).

Best for smaller, more frequent, or automated flow.

<Note>
Both are available over the API and in the terminal. Most institutional flow uses
RFQ; automated strategies more often use streaming prices.
</Note>

<Note>
Execution style is a separate question from **how you reach us**. The same RFQ
can come from the high-touch desk, a chat channel, the portal, or the API — see
[Execution channels](/guides/trading/execution-channels).
</Note>

## What "firm" means

A quote is executable at the stated price for the stated size until
`ValidUntilTime`. Within that window the price will not move against you.

<Warning>
Quotes expire — by default after about three minutes, though `ValidUntilTime` on
the quote is authoritative. An expired quote cannot be accepted; you must request
a new one, and the new price may differ. Build acceptance flows that check expiry
rather than assuming the quote is still live.
</Warning>

## Pricing

Pricing reflects the instrument, size, and your commercial terms. The economics
of a specific trade are on its confirmation: executed price, quantity, fees, and
net settlement amount.

<Note>
Where a fee is charged separately from the price, the all-in economics are on the
[`Trade`](/trading-api/websocket/streams/trade) record, which carries `Fee` and
`Amount` explicitly. `AvgPx` on an execution report excludes fees.
</Note>
