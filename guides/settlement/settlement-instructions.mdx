Standing settlement instructions (SSIs) tell us where your fiat goes. One is
registered per currency and rail, screened, and then used for every settlement in
that currency.

## What an SSI contains

<ResponseField name="Beneficiary" required>
  The legal name on the receiving account. Must match the bank's record exactly.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="Beneficiary address" required>
  **Complete** — street, town, and country. Incomplete addresses are the single
  most common cause of returned wires.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="Account identifier" required>
  Account number, IBAN, or equivalent for the rail.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="Bank" required>
  Receiving bank name, address, and routing identifier — ABA for Fedwire, BIC for
  SWIFT, and so on.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="Intermediary bank">
  Where the receiving bank requires one for international payments.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="Currency and rail" required>
  Which currency and rail this instruction applies to.
</ResponseField>

<Warning>
Both the **beneficiary** address and the **bank** address are required for wire
rails, and both must be structured — street, town, country. A payment can be
refused for a missing town name on either, and the refusal often arrives days
later.
</Warning>

## Registering one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit details">
    Through the platform, or via your account manager. You may be sent a secure
    link to submit them directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verification and screening">
    Details are validated and the beneficiary screened.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approval">
    Once approved the instruction becomes usable for settlement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Registration takes time. Register during onboarding rather than when a trade is
already waiting to settle.
</Note>

## Changing an SSI

Changes are treated as new registrations — re-verified and re-screened, not
edited in place. Allow the same lead time as a first registration.

<Warning>
Bank detail changes are a standard target for payment fraud. We will verify any
change through an independent channel before applying it. Expect that call — and
be suspicious of anyone who tells you it is unnecessary.
</Warning>

## Multiple currencies

Register one instruction per currency you settle in. Instructions are not
interchangeable across currencies, and an instruction registered against one
Trillion Digital entity is not visible to another — see
[Entities](/guides/accounts/entities).

## Payments to third parties

Settlement is to accounts in your own name. Third-party payments require
compliance review in advance and are not accepted as routine.
