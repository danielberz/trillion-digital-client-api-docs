Every trade has two legs. Settlement is the process of moving both against
pre-registered instructions.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Fiat" icon="building-columns" href="/guides/settlement/fiat">
    Bank transfers over Fedwire, SWIFT, ACH, SEPA, or internal book transfer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Crypto" icon="link" href="/guides/settlement/crypto">
    On-chain transfers to whitelisted wallet addresses.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The rule that matters most

<Warning>
**Settlement destinations must be registered and screened before they can be
used.** This applies to both bank accounts and wallet addresses.

Registration is not instant — it involves screening and, for wallets,
verification. A trade that executes before its destination is registered will sit
unsettled until it is. Register destinations during onboarding, not on the day
you need them.
</Warning>

## Prerequisites

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register settlement instructions">
    Bank details for each currency you will settle in. See
    [Settlement instructions](/guides/settlement/settlement-instructions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Whitelist wallet addresses">
    Every crypto destination, per asset and network. See
    [Wallet whitelisting](/guides/settlement/wallet-whitelisting).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the value date">
    Stated on the trade confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ensure funds are in place">
    Your leg must be funded and available — not merely present. See the note on
    available balance below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available versus total balance

Your total balance and your available balance are different numbers.

<ResponseField name="Amount">The total balance held.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="AvailableAmount">
  What you can actually use — total, less funds committed to working orders and
  pending withdrawals.
</ResponseField>

<Warning>
Size trades and withdrawals against `AvailableAmount`. Using the total balance is
a reliable way to have an instruction rejected for insufficient funds while the
account visibly holds enough.
</Warning>

## Cut-off times

Each rail has a daily cut-off, after which an instruction is processed on the
next business day. Cut-offs vary by rail, currency, and jurisdiction, and
business days follow the relevant banking calendar.

<Note>
Cut-off times and holiday calendars applicable to your account are provided in
your onboarding documentation. Confirm them with your account manager rather than
assuming — a missed cut-off on a Friday can mean settlement on Monday.
</Note>

## Tracking settlement

Settlement progress is visible in the platform and, over the API, on the
[`BalanceTransaction`](/trading-api/websocket/streams/balance) stream:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `PendingApproval` | Awaiting internal approval — still cancellable |
| `PendingTransfer` | Approved, being transmitted — no longer cancellable |
| `Completed` | Settled |
| `Rejected` | Not processed; see the accompanying detail |

## If settlement does not arrive

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check status">
    Confirm the instruction is `Completed` rather than pending or rejected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow for the rail">
    Different rails clear on different timescales. Internal book transfers are
    effectively immediate; international wires are not.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the destination">
    Verify the registered destination is the one you expect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact us">
    Email [support@trilliondigital.io](mailto:support@trilliondigital.io) with
    the trade ID and transaction ID.
  </Step>
</Steps>
