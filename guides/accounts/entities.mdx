---

Trillion Digital operates through separate regulated entities in different
jurisdictions. During onboarding you are assigned to one, and that assignment
shapes several practical things.

## What your entity determines

| Area | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Regulatory relationship | Which regime governs the relationship and what protections apply |
| Settlement rails | Which fiat rails are available — see [Fiat settlement](/guides/settlement/fiat) |
| Banking | Which correspondent banking partners hold your funds |
| Documentation | Which terms of business and disclosures apply |
| Trade confirmations | Which entity appears as counterparty on confirmations and statements |

<Note>
Your entity is determined during onboarding based on your jurisdiction,
structure, and the instruments you intend to trade. It is recorded on your
account and appears on every trade confirmation and statement.
</Note>

## Segregation

Entities are operationally segregated. Accounts, balances, settlement
instructions, and trade records belong to one entity and do not commingle.

<Warning>
If your group trades through more than one Trillion Digital entity, each is a
**separate account with separate settlement instructions and separate API
credentials.** Balances are not fungible between them, and a settlement
instruction registered against one is not visible to the other. Confirm which
account you are operating on before instructing a transfer.
</Warning>

## On confirmations and statements

The facing entity is stated on every trade confirmation. If a confirmation shows
an entity you did not expect, raise it with your account manager before settling
— it usually indicates the trade was booked to the wrong account, which is far
easier to correct before settlement than after.
