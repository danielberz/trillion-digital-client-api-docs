---

Onboarding establishes who you are, who ultimately owns and controls the entity,
and which Trillion Digital entity you will face. It must complete before trading.

## What is collected

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Entity documentation">
    Certificate of incorporation, articles or bylaws, proof of registered
    address, and evidence of good standing. Requirements vary by jurisdiction and
    entity type.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ownership and control">
    Ownership structure to the level of ultimate beneficial owners, plus
    identification of directors and authorised signatories. Layered structures
    take longer because each layer is verified.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Individual verification">
    Government-issued identification and proof of address for beneficial owners
    and authorised users, verified through our identity provider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Source of funds and wealth">
    Evidence supporting the origin of the funds being traded, proportionate to
    expected activity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Business rationale">
    Expected volumes, instruments, counterparties, and the commercial purpose of
    the trading activity. This informs ongoing transaction monitoring.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Screening

All entities, beneficial owners, and authorised users are screened against
sanctions, politically-exposed-person, and adverse-media sources at onboarding
and on an ongoing basis thereafter. Wallet addresses are separately screened —
see [Wallet whitelisting](/guides/settlement/wallet-whitelisting).

<Note>
Screening is continuous, not one-off. A change in sanctions status or ownership
after approval can suspend an account. Tell us promptly about changes in
ownership, control, or registered details.
</Note>

## What happens after approval

<Steps>
  <Step title="Account provisioning">
    Your trading account is created against the appropriate Trillion Digital
    entity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="User invitations">
    Authorised users receive email invitations to set credentials and enrol
    multi-factor authentication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settlement registration">
    Bank accounts and wallet addresses are registered and screened.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credentials">
    If you are integrating, API keys are issued per environment on request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keeping onboarding current

Periodic review refreshes documentation on a risk-based cycle. Notify
[compliance@trilliondigital.io](mailto:compliance@trilliondigital.io) of changes
to ownership or control, registered address or entity name, authorised users, or
the nature of your trading activity.

<Warning>
Stale documentation is the most common cause of an otherwise healthy account
being restricted. Reviews are far quicker when documents are supplied before they
are chased.
</Warning>
