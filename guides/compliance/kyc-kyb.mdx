Trillion Digital operates a risk-based AML and counter-terrorist-financing
programme across its regulated entities. Verification is a condition of trading,
not a formality at the start of the relationship.

## At onboarding

Entity verification, beneficial ownership to the ultimate level, individual
identity verification for owners and authorised users, source of funds and
wealth, and expected activity. See [Onboarding](/guides/accounts/onboarding).

## Ongoing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Continuous screening">
    Entities, individuals, and wallet addresses are screened on an ongoing basis
    against sanctions, PEP, and adverse-media sources — not only at onboarding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Transaction monitoring">
    Activity is monitored against expected patterns established at onboarding.
    Activity materially inconsistent with what was described may trigger review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Periodic review">
    Documentation is refreshed on a risk-based cycle. Higher-risk relationships
    are reviewed more frequently.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Blockchain analytics">
    Crypto deposits and withdrawal destinations are screened for exposure to
    illicit activity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What may be asked of you

From time to time we may request updated documentation, an explanation of a
particular transaction, or evidence supporting source of funds.

<Note>
These requests are a normal part of a regulated relationship and are not an
accusation. Responding promptly and completely is the fastest route through —
delays are what turn a routine request into a restriction on the account.
</Note>

## Tell us about changes

Notify [compliance@trilliondigital.io](mailto:compliance@trilliondigital.io)
when ownership or control changes, the entity's name or registered address
changes, authorised users change, or the nature or scale of your activity shifts
materially.

## When activity is restricted

We may decline a transaction, suspend activity, or terminate a relationship where
we are unable to satisfy our obligations.

<Warning>
In some circumstances we are legally prohibited from explaining why a specific
action was taken. This is a constraint of the applicable law, not a matter of
policy or discretion, and it is not something support can work around.
</Warning>
